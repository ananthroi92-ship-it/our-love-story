Poetry For You

Some love is too big for normal sentences, so I kept it here as poetry.

In the soft hum of the world, I find A whisper of your love, gentle and kind. Like the moonlight that dances on the sea, Your heart is where I long to be. In the dark, your eyes shine bright, Guiding me through the deepest night. Nothing's prettier, nothing feels as true, As the soft calm I always find in you. Love's like colors in a rainbow's sky, But in your love, I find colors that make me sigh. Blushing with affection, in a tender hue, Painted by the love I see in you. If Any Word Hurt You

This section is not here to blame you. It is here because love should also know how to repair.

Baby, if anything on this page brings back pain, I want you to know that my intention is not to hurt you. I created this to remember the good, to honor what we shared, and to remind you that beneath every fight, I still see the person I loved, cared for, traveled with, laughed with, and held close.

Some words between us have been painful. Some moments were heavy. But I do not want our story to be remembered only by hurt. I want it to also remember your care, your softness, your strength, your smile, your food, your kisses, your photos, your dresses, your courage, and the way you made ordinary places feel special.

I am not perfect. I may have made mistakes. But this page is my way of saying: I remember the beautiful parts too. I remember you with love.

My Anniversary Letter

To my beloved, Happy anniversary.

I love you beyond words. It is not just a sentence I say; it is an emotion I feel whenever I think of you. I have known you since 2021, and ever since I first proposed on 17 June 2022, that day has lived in my heart as our anniversary.

I love how you look because it reflects the kindness in your soul. There is a calmness in your eyes, the kind that makes me feel seen and safe. Your smile does not just curve your lips; it lights up a room quietly, like a sunrise I never get tired of seeing.

I love you because you have a softness that is not weakness. It is strength wrapped in gentleness. You care in ways that feel warm, real, and rare. You bring comfort simply by being yourself, soft enough to soothe and strong enough to hold space for others.

I love you because you are thoughtful. You ask questions that carry meaning. You seek connection, not just answers. You bring depth with quiet boldness, and you make me feel that love can be both playful and serious, soft and strong, simple and unforgettable.

I enjoyed your care, your smile, your kisses, your food, your selfies, your adventurous side, your Indian dresses, your flight selfie, your hand in the cab, your forehead kiss while I was sleeping, and every small thing you did that made me feel loved.

I just love you, and I can no longer imagine what I would be without you. ❤️
