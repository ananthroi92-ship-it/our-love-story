Poetry For You

Some love is too big for normal sentences, so I kept it here as poetry.

In the soft hum of the world, I find a whisper of your love, gentle and kind. Like moonlight dancing over the sea, your heart is where I long to be. Your laugh turns ordinary days into tiny festivals of light. Your eyes carry a calmness that makes even my storms feel quiet. In the dark, your eyes shine bright, guiding me through the deepest night. Nothing feels more beautiful or true than the way your gaze brings me back to you. We may fight, and storms may start, but love still knocks inside my heart. Before the night can close its eyes, I still want us to heal, not hide. Hand in hand, through thick and thin, through every place our stories begin, no matter what happens, whatever we do, I still want to walk this life with you.

If Any Word Hurt You

This section is not here to blame you. It is here because love should also know how to repair.

Baby, if anything on this page brings back pain, I want you to know that my intention is not to hurt you. I created this to remember the good, to honor what we shared, and to remind you that beneath every fight, I still see the person I loved, cared for, traveled with, laughed with, and held close.

Some words between us have been painful. Some moments were heavy. But I do not want our story to be remembered only by hurt. I want it to also remember your care, your softness, your strength, your smile, your food, your kisses, your photos, your dresses, your courage, and the way you made ordinary places feel special.

I am not perfect. I may have made mistakes. But this page is my way of saying: I remember the beautiful parts too. I remember you with love.

To my beloved, Happy anniversary.

I love you beyond words. It is not just a sentence I say; it is an emotion I feel whenever I think of you. I have known you since 2021, and ever since I first proposed on 17 June 2022, that day has lived in my heart as our anniversary. I love how you look because it reflects the kindness in your soul. There is a calmness in your eyes, the kind that makes me feel seen and safe. Your smile does not just curve your lips; it lights up a room quietly, like a sunrise I never get tired of seeing.

I just love you, and I can no longer imagine what I would be without you. ❤️
